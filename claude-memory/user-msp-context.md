and support clients remotely, with the **primary client base in Kuwait** (the original brief
also mentions Canada→Qatar). Target machines are client-owned Windows 10/11 boxes the
business has no prior control over.

Strongly cost-sensitive — pushed hard for a $0 hosting option and needed to be walked
through why $0.80/month was the floor before accepting it. Prefers concrete numbers over
architecture discussion.

Writes quickly with heavy typos and thinks out loud. Not a signal of low technical skill:
the underlying instincts are often sound (proposed multi-source segmented download, which
is a real technique used by aria2 and BitTorrent), and they respond well to being told
*why* an idea breaks rather than just that it does.
